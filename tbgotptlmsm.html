Sibyl
The Sibyl of my beating heart screeched at me: “You, boy, will find her. Be patient.” I tried my hardest to believe What the Sibyl said, Follow her guidance Loneliness kept creeping closer And closer To the Sibyl, Until the week That still rings and dances In my bones Arrived She wailed at me She was going to die I was ready for the release, To never see the sweet Sibyl Again When another joined the fight In a fraction of a Of a second Of a twitch Of a moment Another woman saved my Sibyl The woman slayed My loneliness with Pure affection, And quickly too Her Sibyl was even closer To death than mine; Yet she helped me Without even trying That is just how the woman was That is just how the woman is Nowadays, I am still a lunatic A manic A weirdo; But one with a Purpose I want to watch The dear woman Who saved me Grow old, Her body withering, With both of our Sibyls Strolling together As young and healthy And in love as ever
~ toma b.
